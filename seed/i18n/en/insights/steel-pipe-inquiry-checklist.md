An inquiry that only says "8-inch pipe" can refer to dozens of products at completely different prices. The more complete the specification, the more accurate, faster and more comparable the offers will be.

## 1. Standard and grade

The standard defines the pipe's chemical, mechanical and testing requirements. The most common are:

- **API 5L** for oil and gas transmission lines; grades B to X70 and specification level PSL1 or PSL2.
- **ASTM A106** for high-temperature service in process piping (Gr.B and Gr.C).
- **ASTM A53** for general, water and structural applications.
- **ASTM A333** for low-temperature service.

If you have the project specification (piping class or line list), use it as the reference.

## 2. Diameter and wall thickness

The nominal size (NPS) alone is not enough; specify the wall thickness by **schedule (SCH)** or the exact **WT** in millimetres. For example, an 8-inch pipe is about 8.18 mm thick in SCH 40 and about 12.7 mm in SCH 80 — a difference that directly affects weight and price.

## 3. Manufacturing type

- **Seamless** for high pressure and temperature.
- **Welded (ERW, LSAW or SSAW)**, which is usually more economical for larger diameters.

If the project specification requires a manufacturing type, be sure to state it.

## 4. Pipe ends, length and coating

- **Ends:** plain (PE), bevelled for welding (BE) or threaded (TE).
- **Length:** single random length (SRL), double random length (DRL) or fixed length.
- **Coating:** bare, paint or varnish, galvanized, or anti-corrosion coatings for buried lines such as 3LPE and FBE.

## 5. Quantity, delivery point and timing

State the quantity in metres or tonnes and specify the delivery point (warehouse, mill or project site). The required delivery time also affects the supply options.

## 6. Documents and inspection

Is an **EN 10204 type 3.1 MTC** sufficient, or does the project require **3.2** and third-party inspection? Also state any additional tests, such as hydrostatic, UT or impact testing.

---

**Summary:** standard and grade, diameter and wall thickness, manufacturing type, ends and coating, quantity and delivery point, and the required documents. If you have a material take-off (MTO), attach it to the inquiry form.
